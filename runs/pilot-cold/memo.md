# Recommendation
Prioritize **usability** next quarter, attacking ISS-usability-redesign_ui (Missing Playback Controls and Settings) first, followed by ISS-usability-ads (Frequent and Loud Ads). Focus on making repeat, seek, and automatic-playback settings discoverable and investigating ad loudness and frequency.

## Why this area
Usability has the strongest aggregate signal among the eligible areas: 15 complaint records, 28.8% of complaints, and an aggregate severity sum of 37. This supports concentrating effort on everyday listening friction rather than selecting solely on the highest-ranked individual issue.

The actionable starting point is ISS-usability-redesign_ui (Missing Playback Controls and Settings): 5 complaints [C009], mean severity 2.800000 [C011], and priority score 14 [C012]. Reports identify concrete difficulties with repeat, seeking, and disabling automatic playback. These are suitable targets for focused usability investigation, though the reviews do not establish whether controls are missing, hard to find, or unavailable under particular conditions.

ISS-usability-ads (Frequent and Loud Ads) contributes 5 complaints [C017], mean severity 2.000000 [C019], and priority score 10 [C020]. ISS-usability-general (Mixed Playback and Ad Complaints) adds 4 complaints [C021], mean severity 2.500000 [C023], and priority score 10 [C024]. The latter reinforces the friction signal but needs clarification before becoming a specific delivery commitment.

## Alternatives considered
**Billing/support** has 8 complaint records and aggregate severity 23. Its leading issue, ISS-billing-premium_only_controls (Basic Controls Require Premium), has 6 complaints [C005], mean severity 3.000000 [C007], and priority score 18 [C008]. It outranks any individual usability issue, but concerns subscription boundaries rather than a demonstrated defect. Evidence on plan economics and customer expectations could change the decision.

**Playback** also has 8 complaint records, with aggregate severity 27. ISS-playback-crashes_wont_open (App Crashes or Gets Stuck) has 3 complaints [C013], mean severity 3.666667 [C015], and priority score 11 [C016]. Its higher severity merits parallel diagnostic attention; evidence of widespread failures would justify reprioritization.

**Access** has 2 complaint records and aggregate severity 8, giving it the weakest observed volume signal. Outside the eligible areas, ISS-other-general (Mixed Generic App Complaints) leads with 13 complaints [C001], mean severity 2.153846 [C003], and priority score 28 [C004], but lacks a specific defect to attack.

## Limitations
These self-selected public reviews are not representative prevalence estimates. The pack covers 100 completed classifications, with 0 unclassified records; it reports no unresolved classification records. There is no revenue, plan, or churn data, and cancellation language is expressed intent, not confirmed churn.

Priority equals complaint count multiplied by mean severity, with one issue assigned per complaint; overlapping concerns may therefore be obscured. Verification covered 20 records: intent agreement 0.95, topic agreement 0.9, severity agreement 0.95, and severity MAE 0.05. Label uncertainty remains, and the evidence does not establish causes or expected results of fixes.
